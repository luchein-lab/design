# The Capptus Way

Created October 1, 2026 from the user-confirmed delivery stages and the approved Capptus line-art direction. The original corporate Capptus logo is preserved.

| Narrative | Operational stage | Meaning | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seedling | Discover | Understand the problem, goals, users, processes, data, constraints and expected outcomes. | Official stage |
| Budding | Design | Design journeys, architecture, data, integrations, experience and backlog. | Official stage |
| Growing | Build | Configure, develop and build the solution. | Official stage |
| Nurturing | Integrate | Connect systems, data, channels and ecosystem components. | Official stage |
| Pruning | Test / Train | QA, UAT, corrections, operational readiness, training and adoption. | Official stage |
| Harvest | Deploy | Go-live, stabilization and capture of expected value. | Official stage |
| Regrowth / Rebrote | Optimize / Grow | Measure, learn and improve after go-live; feed findings into Discover and Design. | Proposed extension; narrative name proposed |

The user suggested an explicit post-deployment stage for Agentforce, Managed Services and Marketing Cloud as a Service. This extension is presented for review, rather than retroactively treated as part of the official six-stage sequence.

## Illustration language

Use predominant near-black organic outlines, generous open interiors, and small blue and sand accents. Keep the botanical silhouettes distinct: seedling, closed bud, growing structure, connected cacti, pruning scissors, ripe fruits, and new growth with a return arrow.

The seven originals are transparent 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNGs. They are raster editorial illustrations for decks, section introductions and documents. Keep their aspect ratios, preserve alpha, and place them on white or limestone. Around 96px or larger is a practical starting point, not a certified minimum. Tiny UI controls need simpler vector glyphs.
