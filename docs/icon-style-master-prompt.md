# Master Prompt — Icons in the Capptus Style

Create **[NUMBER] Capptus-style icons** representing **[SUBJECTS / CONCEPTS]**, for use in **[DECK / WEBSITE / CUSTOMER DOCUMENT / FLOWCHART / OTHER]**.

**Visual mode:** [REGULAR / NEGATIVE / BOTH]  
**Detail level:** [COMPACT ICON / EDITORIAL SYMBOL]  
**Background:** [TRANSPARENT / LIMESTONE / CAPPTUS BLUE]

## Brand character

Capptus is rooted in **Baja, the desert, nature, and the resilience of the cactus**. Its visual language is warm, editorial, expressive, and sophisticated.

Translate this character into clear, distinctive line art:

- Predominantly dark outlines, softly rounded terminals, and gently irregular curves.

- Simple, slightly asymmetric silhouettes with generous open interiors.

- A few confident strokes and restrained accents of color.

- Flat artwork with personality, suitable for professional communication.

Use the approved Capptus Way illustrations and icons as the primary visual reference when supplied. Preserve their balance of organic shapes, simplicity, and clarity.

**The cactus inspires the drawing language; it does not need to appear in every icon.** Choose imagery that makes the subject immediately understandable.

## Shape and construction

Build each icon around **one dominant idea**. Use one primary silhouette and, when necessary, one supporting element.

Favor elongated curves, rounded branches, soft geometric forms, and purposeful negative space. Keep slight asymmetry controlled and intentional. Avoid perfectly mechanical stock-icon geometry or excessively loose sketching.

Keep consistent visual weight, optical size, margins, and detail across the entire set. Small details must remain legible at the intended display size.

For compact icons:

- Design for approximately 32–96 px display.

- Use a square canvas with comfortable margins.

- Prefer continuous outlines and minimal internal detail.

For functional UI symbols:

- Use a 24 × 24 grid with approximately 2 px strokes.

- Use rounded caps and joins.

- Preserve familiar, readable conventions for actions such as search, download, close, and navigation.

## Regular style

Use on white or warm limestone backgrounds.

**Palette**

- Primary linework: Ink **#1B1B19**

- Main accent: Capptus Blue **#008ED1**

- Secondary accent: Sand **#D8C5AB**

- Background: Limestone **#F8F7F3** or white **#FFFFFF**

Keep most interiors open or transparent. Use blue for one meaningful gesture, connection, emerging element, or focal detail. Sand may provide a small grounding shape.

Color should support the silhouette, never overwhelm it.

## Negative style

Use on a solid **Capptus Blue #008ED1** background, or deliver transparent artwork intended for that background.

**Palette**

- Primary linework: White **#FFFFFF**

- Accent: Sand **#D8C5AB**

- Open interiors: transparent, revealing the blue background

Translate the regular version into white linework with sparse sand accents. Elements that would disappear against the blue background must become white or sand.

Preserve the same silhouette, proportions, spacing, and visual weight. Adjust small details only when needed for clarity.

Do not add black outlines, shadows, glow, or a white container behind the icon. Negative artwork should feel like a deliberate member of the same family.

## Adaptation by use case

**Industry symbols:** Use recognizable elements from the industry—such as a storefront, factory, healthcare cross, financial exchange, or logistics route—drawn with the Capptus line language. Create original sector symbols; use supplied official artwork when an actual company logo is required.

**Flowchart concepts:** Make the concept recognizable in isolation and consistent with adjacent steps. Keep external connectors separate from the icon. Use connections inside the icon only when they express its meaning.

**Abstract concepts:** Translate ideas into concrete visual metaphors: connected forms for integration, a trimmed branch for refinement, emerging growth for progress, or a circular return gesture for continuous improvement.

## Exclusions

No 3D, volume, gradients, airbrushing, grain, textures, glossy effects, drop shadows, detailed backgrounds, decorative clutter, cute faces, or unnecessary cactus elements.

No text, captions, letters, numbers, or logos unless explicitly requested. If typography is required, use **Lora Medium 500** for editorial titles and **Montserrat 400/500/600** for labels and UI.

## Deliverables

Produce:

1. A clearly arranged preview of the complete set.
2. Each icon individually, consistently sized and centered.
3. Matching regular and negative versions when **BOTH** is selected.

For vector workflows, provide native SVG paths with editable strokes. For image-generation workflows, provide high-resolution transparent PNGs; do not describe raster artwork as vector.
