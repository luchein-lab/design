CONSTRUCTION
UI: 24x24 grid, 2.2 stroke, round cap/join, 20/24/32 display sizes, 44x44 target.
Compact: 64x64 grid, 3.2 stroke, 32 minimum, 48 preferred, 8 px surrounding clear space.
Full artwork: preferably 128px or larger, never squeeze or crop.
Full illustration PNGs have inherited tonal variations; compact SVGs use exact tokens.
SVG strokes scale with their viewBox. Do not add non-scaling-stroke.

ACCESSIBILITY
Decorative image beside the same text: alt="". Meaningful standalone image: localized alt describing its purpose.
Icon-only button: aria-label with its action; nested SVG aria-hidden="true".
Never rely on blue, motion or the botanical picture alone for a status. Keep status in explicit text.
Interface SVGs use currentColor when inline. An external img cannot inherit the surrounding CSS color.
Keep illustrated assets in their original colors. Use the light-tile PNG on dark or unknown chat surfaces.

MOTION
One 240ms response; maximum scale 1.04. Reduced-motion mode is static. No idle loops.

SHORTCODES
Names such as :capptus-bloom: are proposed custom emoji names. They are not Unicode or installed in Slack, Teams or WhatsApp. Follow each destination's current upload requirements before importing; supplied files are general-purpose exports.

PROVENANCE
Original 8 illustrations were generated earlier with the built-in image tool and are preserved byte for byte. Logo is the user-supplied original. New compact SVGs and interface symbols are code-native vector artwork; the compact versions simplify the original motifs and are not vectorizations of the PNGs. No new image-generation model or outside brand assets were used.
