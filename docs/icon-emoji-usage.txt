USAGE
Illustrations: use 128px or larger, keep full silhouettes and aspect ratios.
Compact icons: use 32–96px on white or Limestone (#F8F7F3). These are hand-drawn
vector interpretations of the motifs, not automatic traces of the illustrations.
The viewBox is -5 -5 110 110, including safety margin; main strokes are 4 units,
with reduced detail at 2–3.5 units and round caps/joins.
Interface icons: use 20–32px inside labeled controls with 44px minimum hit targets.
SVG masters use currentColor; default black, blue available for active states.
Use darker action blue #006D9E where interactive contrast requires it.
Negative primary logo: use the blue plate or transparent white/sand master on a suitable dark surface.
Do not place botanical black linework on dark surfaces. Do not add shadows or stretch assets.
Always write stage meanings and statuses as text; illustrations do not replace labels.

CREATING NEW ICONS
Use docs/icon-style-master-prompt.md (master-prompt.md inside the kit).
Regular: ink lines with small blue/sand accents on white or limestone.
Negative: white lines with sparse sand accents on Capptus blue; open interiors.
Preserve silhouettes and visual weight. Convert disappearing blue details to white or sand.
Use industry subjects and clear conceptual metaphors; a cactus is not required in every icon.
Keep artwork flat: no volume, gradients, shadows, textures or faces.
The prompt defines future production rules; existing negative icon variants are not implied.
